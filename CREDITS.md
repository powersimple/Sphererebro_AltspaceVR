## Made for the talk (no third-party content)

- **Every 3D model** is procedural, generated by the scripts in `tools/blender/` (Blender 5.2).
- **Every generated image** in `talk/gen/` is drawn by those scripts: server faces, the radio dial, the TV glow, the 1990s
  web page (grey text bars, no words or branding), feed-tile pictures (abstract gradients), book spines, gallery paintings
  (abstract), the bill's ruled paper, and the environment gradients.
- **Every sound** in `Assets/_Sphererebro/Talk/Audio/` is synthesised by `tools/talk/sounds.py` (numpy): the ambient beds,
  whooshes, the rack landing, rumble, stamp thud, paper, odometer, water, shatter, chimes and swells.
- **Fonts:** Inter (SIL Open Font License 1.1, https://rsms.me/inter/), Aldrich (SIL OFL 1.1, Google Fonts), Bahnschrift
  (installed with Windows; used only to generate the stamp-ink words as meshes).
- **Placeholder clips** in `media/`: rendered by ffmpeg from the world's own Milky Way skybox plate
  (`Assets/_Sphererebro/Skybox/`).
